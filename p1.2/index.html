User Scenario Mapping

Scenario:

Sakura, a 25-year-old marketing professional, recently started incorporating matcha into her daily routine for its health benefits. She wants to explore high-quality matcha options and the right tools to prepare it. While browsing online, she finds Green Whisk and is intrigued by its organic selection. She explores the website to learn more about different blends and accessories before making a purchase.

Actor:

Sakura

Motivator:

Wants to find high-quality matcha and tools to enhance her tea-making experience.

Intent:

To purchase premium organic matcha and accessories to improve her matcha preparation process.

Action:

Sakura browses through Green Whisk’s product categories, reads descriptions and customer reviews, adds her selected matcha blend and a whisk to her cart, and proceeds to checkout.

Resolution:

Sakura successfully purchases matcha and accessories, feeling excited to elevate her daily tea ritual with Green Whisk's premium products.
